This worklog documents the learning, practical activities, and project development carried out during the **12-week First Cloud AI Journey program**, from **11/05/2026 to 31/07/2026**.

The first three weeks focused on building a foundation in AWS services, serverless architecture, Machine Learning, and Docker. From Week 4 to Week 10, these skills were further developed through studying NLP models, deploying a frontend application on AWS, monitoring services with CloudWatch, containerizing applications with Docker, integrating a Machine Learning model with AWS Lambda, and completing the end-to-end system workflow. The final two weeks focused on data preparation, model training, React UI development, frontend-backend integration, and completing the **Toxic Text Moderation Platform** demo. The program concluded with the completion of the final report and the handover of the system components on **31/07/2026**.

The **Off-Campus Internship (TTNT, Semester 253)** course at Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology was registered for the period from **15/06/2026 to 14/08/2026**. Weeks 1–5 of this worklog covered the self-study and preparation phase completed before the official internship period began in Week 6. After submitting the report on **31/07/2026**, the team continued collecting feedback, improving, and further developing the system until **14/08/2026**, as presented in Section 7.
